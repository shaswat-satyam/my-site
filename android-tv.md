## Youtube
The basic android tv Youtube app is riddled with ads. Every 5 mins of watch time, you have an ad. Ads even in Feeds. Worst, if you listen to some music.
When you are having someone over the worst thing is getting ads which you are watching your favourite youtubers. This is where SmartTube comes

### SmartTube
This is an alternative youtube app which has inbuilt adblock and sponsorblock.
So you get rid of ads in the feeds and mid stream.
Also if you watching videos which have some sponsorships like intro, outro or product promo this app skips them. 
Saving on time and data.
Setting this up is very easy similar to the actual youtube app, you have to scan a QR Code and sign up by mobile device.
All your subscriptions, recommendations also carry over.
Highly Recommended, If I get a new TV, I will install this.

## Cable TV
If you have elders or parents, who prefer to watch the cable TV but you can not afford or don't want cable TV.

### UniTV
You can install Unitv which will fetch the various cable channels all over the world.
So you watch world wide cable TV or can filter for you desired country's TV.
The various channels work quite well.

But sometimes there is a youtube link to the actual channel as some channels have a live Youtube stream or some very niche channels are not there but it works the best no cost.
