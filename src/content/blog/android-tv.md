## Youtube
![Youtube TV](https://external-content.duckduckgo.com/iu/?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftse3.mm.bing.net%2Fth%2Fid%2FOIP.QBO21q8n_XHbr7Ch1HRJ_wHaFI%3Fpid%3DApi&f=1&ipt=1710629dd71a51a4de4c2d83121c9e163ecbe1a742e939d08051c498a9213e41&ipo=images)

The basic Android TV Youtube app is riddled with ads. Every 5 mins of watch time, you have an ad. Ads even in Feeds. If you listen to some music, you are not going to enjoy it.
When you are having someone over the worst thing is getting ads which you are watching your favourite youtubers. This is where SmartTube comes

### [SmartTubeNext](https://smarttubenext.org/)
![Smarttube App](https://smarttubenext.org/assets/2.webp)

This is an alternative youtube app which has inbuilt adblock and sponsorblock.
So you get rid of ads in the feeds and mid stream.
Also if you watching videos which have some sponsorships like intro, outro or product promo this app skips them automatically. 
Saving on your time and data.
Setting this up is very easy similar to the actual youtube app, you have to scan a QR Code and sign up by mobile device.
All your subscriptions, recommendations also carry over.
**Highly Recommended**, If I get a new TV, I will install this.
